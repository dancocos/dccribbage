Briana (I hope I spelled that right) was undefeatable last week and had a 24 point hand.

Cribbage, like many games, has local and family customs. Knob, nibs vs the right jack, see one play one. Counting a zero point hand as 19, or in the custom of DC Cribbage a BOD. One such tradition is muggins, which has the opposing team taking the points you missed. Since DC Cribbage is friendly, we do not play with muggins, which is the only optional rule allowed by the American Cribbage Congress.

>Muggins will be permitted only if so stated on the tournament flyer, and must
apply to both the qualifying round and the playoffs. Muggins in the playoffs only
is not permitted.
> - <cite>Rule 1.6</cite>
